     OUR SPEAKING NOTES

    Let's talk
    about apples.

    Hello. Our project is about BC apples. Apples first came from Central Asia. In 1859, Father Pandosy planted apple trees near Kelowna.

    Today, many BC apples grow in the Okanagan and Similkameen valleys. They grow outside in orchards. Bees help the flowers. Farmers usually harvest apples from August to October.

    One fun fact is that Ambrosia apples were found in BC. Apples also contain a lot of water.

    Apples have fibre and some vitamin C. We think they are a good school snack. They are easy to carry and need no cooking. Buying local apples helps BC farmers.

    Our three recipes are an apple yogurt bowl, an apple carrot salad and a warm cinnamon apple.

    Now let's try three questions. Thank you for listening!
